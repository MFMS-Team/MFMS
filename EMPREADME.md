\# Employee Management Module



\## Overview



The Employee Management Module is responsible for maintaining employee information within the Municipal Financial Management System (MFMS).



The module demonstrates:



\- Arrays

\- Parallel Arrays

\- Strings

\- Functions

\- Loops

\- Conditional Statements

\- Input Validation



\---



\## Features



\### Add Employee



Capture employee information:



\- Employee ID

\- Full Name

\- Gender

\- Marital Status

\- Department

\- Position

\- Phone Number

\- Email Address

\- Employment Status

\- Date Hired

\- Basic Salary

\- Housing Allowance

\- Transport Allowance



\---



\### Display Employees



Displays a summary of all employees.



Information displayed:



\- Employee ID

\- Full Name

\- Department

\- Position

\- Employment Status



\---



\### Search Employee



Employees can be searched by:



\- Employee ID

\- Employee Full Name



Complete employee details are displayed when found.



\---



\### Update Employee



Allows modification of:



\- Full Name

\- Gender

\- Marital Status

\- Department

\- Position

\- Phone Number

\- Email Address

\- Employment Status

\- Date Hired

\- Basic Salary

\- Housing Allowance

\- Transport Allowance



\---



\### Delete Employee



Removes employee records from the system while maintaining parallel array consistency.



\---



\### Sort Employees



Sorts employee records alphabetically by employee name.



All related arrays are sorted together.



\---

\## Validation Features



The system validates:



\- Employee ID Numbers

\- Full Names

\- Phone Numbers

\- Email Addresses

\- Date Hired

\- Department Selection

\- Position Selection

\- Marital Status Selection

\- Employment Status Selection



\---



\## Date Validation



The system validates:



\- Leap Years

\- Month Ranges

\- Day Ranges

\- February (28/29 days)

\- Months containing 30 days

\- Months containing 31 days
